How fullscreen works in the Tesla

The car’s browser normally sits in half the screen and won’t expand on its own. The workaround every launcher site uses — including the paid ones — is a YouTube redirect: YouTube is allowed to open fullscreen in the car, and its redirect link carries you onward to the app you actually want, still fullscreen.

1

Open this page in the car

Load Nate’s Theater in the Tesla browser and bookmark it, so it’s always one tap from the browser’s favorites.

2

Tap “Fullscreen” on an app

That button routes through youtube.com/redirect to the app’s real address. You may see a brief YouTube “leave site” prompt — confirm it.

3

Watch on the whole screen

The app opens filling the display. To get back, use the browser’s back button or re-open this bookmark. Use “Open” instead when you want the normal split view.

📱 About iPhone screen mirroring

Honest answer: a web page can’t mirror your iPhone to the Tesla screen. Tesla doesn’t support AirPlay, and mirroring apps work by running their own receiver software — not something a bookmark can do.

What does work is what’s on this page: open the service directly in the car browser. For anything that lives only on your phone, the car browser is the workaround, not the mirror.

🔐 Signing in

Plex, YouTube, and Netflix each ask you to sign in the first time, right inside the car browser. That sign-in goes straight to their own sites — this page never sees or stores your passwords.
